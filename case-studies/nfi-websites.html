The problem

NFI Solutions needed a recruiting website for insurance agents. Its sister brand, NFI Retirement Academy, needed to educate everyday clients and eventually support agent seminar training. Two audiences, two intents, and one shared parent brand had to become a coherent system.

Design strategy

Opportunity versus education

NFI Solutions had to answer why an agent should join the organization. NFI Retirement Academy had to feel trustworthy and educational to people arriving with retirement questions. The goal was to teach, not sell.

I preserved shared structural logic and reusable templates so the brands feel related and the system can be maintained after handoff.

65% increase

Organic search visibility increased 65%, contributing to an estimated 50% to 70% increase in overall website traffic.

Full ownership

Outside of the original logo, I controlled page design, architecture, templates, copy structure, lead-capture forms, automation connections, domain decisions, plugins, and integrations. I built reusable patterns rather than one-off pages so future additions would remain consistent.

Outcome

The NFI Solutions redesign is live and measurable. NFI Retirement Academy remains in its pre-launch build phase, but already provides a shared foundation for client education and future agent training without requiring a separate design system.

What this demonstrates: designing for different audiences under one brand umbrella, scalable template systems, technical SEO, and complete ownership from infrastructure through interface.
